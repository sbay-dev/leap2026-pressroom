## English

During LEAP and DeepFest 2026 week, SBAY presents an independent brief around
two flagship products: **NewsBoy**, a rights-aware Arabic news discovery
experience, and **Ksar**, a commerce and live-market experience. The page also
shows how the small-footprint operations experiment at `cp.sbay.sa` supports
these products, alongside a bounded public record of Sarmad and CNS work.

No model weights, training data, expert maps, credentials, or unpublished
patent enablement are included. Embedding results are stated at their measured
boundary: task-specific wins on XQuAD and Tatoeba, a loss on Sadeem, and no
claim of general MTEB superiority.

This is independent participation, not a statement of official sponsorship or
partnership with LEAP, DeepFest, or their organizers. Any discussion of SBAY
brand licensing or technology services in a Class 42 context remains subject
to legal due diligence and a signed agreement.
