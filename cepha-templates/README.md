# Cepha visual templates

These files preserve reusable visual treatments independently from the
evidence published by the LEAP pressroom.

## Available templates

- `k-space-concept-v1`: interactive illuminated saddle, network anchors,
  concept path, proof console, and gold replay cursor.

Every template must retain its own claim boundary. A visual template is not a
measurement of a model, its weights, its activations, or its geometry.
